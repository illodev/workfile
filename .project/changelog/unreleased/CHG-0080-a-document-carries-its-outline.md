Documents in the reader now list their headings beside the prose, with the section on screen marked and one click to reach any other — the spec's 162 headings included. Rendered headings carry stable anchors, which is also what a link to a section needs.

The rail yields on a narrow viewport, and a document with nothing to outline never grows one.
